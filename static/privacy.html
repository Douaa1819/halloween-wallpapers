Privacy Policy

Cute Halloween Wallpapers · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Cute Halloween Wallpapers is a free app with wallpapers you can browse, save and set as your phone wallpaper. This page explains what information the app uses. "We" means the developer of Cute Halloween Wallpapers.

In short: there are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email or location. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to do that, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your favourites, the list of wallpapers you downloaded, your light or dark theme choice, and a saved copy of the wallpaper list.
  • Wallpapers you download are saved to your gallery, or applied as your wallpaper when you choose "Set Wallpaper".

This stays on your phone and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. The app does not read your other photos and does not upload anything.

Information collected when the app loads content

To show wallpapers, the app downloads the wallpaper list and images from our hosting on Google Firebase Hosting. As with any website, the hosting service may record technical information such as your IP address and device details in standard server logs. We don't use this to identify you.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, full-screen and app-open ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device and app identifiers,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your device, the app and how you interact with ads.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. You can review your choice later in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Where consent is required and not given, the app does not request ads.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ads personalisation in your phone's settings (usually under Privacy → Ads).

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet: to load wallpapers and ads.
  • Photos / media: to save wallpapers to your gallery.
  • Set wallpaper: to apply a wallpaper to your home or lock screen.
  • Vibration: for tap feedback.
  • Advertising ID: so Google can show and measure ads.

Libraries used by the app may add other standard permissions, such as checking network status. We don't use them to collect personal information.

Children

Cute Halloween Wallpapers is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help you with it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information. The only data that leaves your device is what is described above: requests to our hosting, and data handled by Google AdMob.

Keeping and deleting data

We don't keep accounts or personal profiles. Data stored by the app on your phone can be removed by uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or using Settings → Clear downloaded images in the app. Data held by Google for advertising is covered by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
